Implementation rules

  • Rebuild at a desktop content width that preserves the approved proportions rather than stretching horizontally.
  • All icons must stay square and use aspect-ratio:1; never stretch SVGs or raster icons.
  • Use light blue translucent glass cards with thin white/blue borders, soft blur, inner highlights, and restrained blue shadows.
  • Keep the overall page light blue—not white, and not dark navy.
  • Preserve equal widths/heights within each repeated card row.
  • Images use object-fit:cover or contain depending on the approved reference; never distort.
  • The final implementation should recreate the artwork with semantic HTML/CSS/JS/React. This prototype intentionally uses the approved rendered artwork to guarantee a 1:1 visual checkpoint.
